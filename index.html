    About the data

    How builder matches are identified

    This view matches King County home addresses to builder-related company names in county sales records. A match can reflect a home sale, land sale, or parcel transfer, so it may not identify the builder of the current structure.

    Matched address
    The address and parcel number match King County property data.
    Builder match
    A reviewed builder-company name appears in a county sale associated with the parcel.
    Year built
    The construction year listed by King County; it is not the date of the builder-company match.
    Data notes
    Date, classification, or multi-parcel details that may need a closer look.
    County data and coverage

    County records included: 1976–September 4, 2026. Coverage varies by builder, and 2026 is incomplete.

    Data dates will appear when the homes load.

    King County data source ↗
    About this project & contact

    Who Built My Home? is an independent project maintained by whobuiltmyhome on GitHub. It is not affiliated with King County or the builders listed here.

    Report a data issue or contact the project ↗. GitHub sign-in is required and reports are public. Please do not include private documents or personal contact details.

    Privacy

    Address searches and filters run in your browser against the site's downloaded data. Google Analytics is currently disabled. Searches can appear in your browser's URL and history; the Copy home link button shares only the selected parcel.

    Hosting providers may receive ordinary request information. Opening the map loads tiles from OpenStreetMap, which receives requests for the area displayed. External links open third-party services with their own privacy policies.
