    Read the record, understand the limits

    A connection is a starting point.

    We connect verified property addresses with historical company-sale evidence. A company may have sold land, transferred a parcel, or been connected to an earlier structure. Those facts need separate review before naming the builder of today’s home.

    Verified address
    The released address has been matched to county property records.
    Historical company connection
    A company transaction is associated with the parcel. It is not a construction certificate.
    Builder unconfirmed
    The original builder of the current structure has not been established by this release.
    Review flags
    Specific gaps or conflicts that need attention. Having no listed flag does not confirm the builder.

    Research window: 1976–2026. This is a research goal, not a claim of complete fifty-year coverage. Earlier source evidence is preserved, and 2026 is incomplete.

    Release information will appear when the collection loads.
