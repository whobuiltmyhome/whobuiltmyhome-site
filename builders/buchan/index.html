Read the company evidence and dates

Open a parcel's evidence panel to see its reviewed company label and recording references. A company in a sale record may have been a seller, owner, or developer. Check the original construction permit before treating that association as confirmation of the contractor.

The county source snapshot is dated September 4, 2026. Included parcels have first matching company recordings from January 6, 1976 to August 26, 2026. Their current county year-built values range from 1965 to 2026. Recording and construction dates describe different events, and this range is not a company operating history.

Verify a particular home's builder

Search an exact address or ten-digit parcel number, open the county property report, and compare the cited document in the official Recorder's search ↗. Check the legal description, company names, and dates against the original permit from the correct city or county office.

This collection contains 52 parcel associations supported only by land-classified sales without an improved-residential classification, and 18 whose county construction year is at least three years after the earliest matching recording. Some flags overlap. They identify evidence to inspect rather than establish an error or a different builder.

A missing result means this release has no reviewed association for that parcel; it does not rule out a Buchan connection. Use the King County builder research guide to continue with official records and older documents.

Sources and scope

Counts and dates are computed from the released county parcel, residential-building, and sale-record data. See the matching methodology and official county property-research tools ↗ for individual verification. This independent collection is not affiliated with or endorsed by the builders named here.
