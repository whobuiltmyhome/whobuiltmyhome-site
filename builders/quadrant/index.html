Keep the recording date separate from year built

Among included parcels, the earliest first matching company recording is March 31, 1982, and the latest is December 30, 2020. The underlying county source snapshot is dated September 4, 2026, but this collection's reviewed company matches stop in 2020. Misspellings, other entities, combined sellers, and transactions from 2021 onward await review.

County year-built values range from 1979 to 2026. A construction year after the company sale does not establish that Quadrant built that structure. Resolve the property's chronology with the original documents and permits.

What to check before naming the builder

Open a parcel in the explorer and follow its county property link. Use its recording references in the official Recorder's search ↗, then compare the legal description, company name, and dates. A property seller can differ from the contractor who constructed the home.

For this collection, 1,329 associations have only land-classified supporting sales rather than an improved-residential classification. Another review flag marks 92 parcels where county year built is at least three years after the earliest matching recording. There are also 1,265 associations with a recording covering multiple parcels. Flags can overlap, and these counts are prompts for document review, not proven errors.

Ask the correct city or county permit office for the original construction file. Check its named contractor and project description against the parcel. Our King County builder research guide explains how to find the jurisdiction and work through older recorded documents.
